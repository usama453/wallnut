Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

1. Who we are

This policy explains how AI Proof (“we”) collects and handles data when you use Wallnut, our AI proofing service on WhatsApp, or visit this website.

2. What we collect

  • Contact details — your WhatsApp number and name.
  • Content you submit — images, PDFs and text messages you send to Wallnut for proofing.
  • Proof results — the score and list of issues we return, and any approvals you make.
  • Usage data — basic information about how the service is used, such as timestamps and feature usage.

3. How we use your data

  • to provide the proofing service and reply to you on WhatsApp;
  • to store and display your proof history in the AI Proof dashboard;
  • to improve the service, fix bugs and keep it secure; and
  • to respond to your questions or requests.

We do not sell your personal data, your content, or the proof results, and we do not use them for advertising.

4. Legal basis

We process your data to perform the service you asked for (contract), to pursue our legitimate interests in running and improving the service, and, where required, on the basis of your consent which you may withdraw at any time.

5. Who we share data with

To run the service we work with a small number of processors:

  • WhatsApp (Meta) — to receive and send messages through the WhatsApp Business Platform;
  • AI providers — to analyse your images and PDFs and produce proof results;
  • Cloud providers — to store content and results and host the dashboard and this website.

Each processor only receives the data needed to perform its role and is required to keep it secure.

6. How long we keep data

We keep submitted content and proof results only for as long as needed to provide the service, after which they are deleted. You can ask us to delete your data at any time using the contact details below.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct or update it;
  • ask us to delete it;
  • object to or restrict certain processing; and
  • withdraw any consent you have given.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. You also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

8. Security

We use encryption in transit and reasonable access controls to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to keep your data safe.

9. Cookies

This website is a simple static page and does not set cookies. The AI Proof dashboard may use local storage or session mechanisms for login — please see that app’s own notices for details.

10. Children

The service is not directed at children and is not intended for users under 16. If we learn we have collected a child’s data, we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page.

12. Contact

Privacy questions or requests? Message us on WhatsApp at hey@usama.fun · WhatsApp.
